# Product Requirements Document (PRD): BFPS Brand-Aligned Lead Generation Funnel

## 1. Overview & Objective
**Project:** Premium Digital Lead Generation Funnel for Bright Future Public School (BFPS).
**Primary Goal:** Capture contact details of nearby parents (Pincode: 302004) to bridge the enrollment gap from the current 200 students to the 400-student capacity.
**Strategic Imperative:** Due to high staff turnover, all messaging and imagery must focus strictly on the *institutional philosophy*, the *proprietary curriculum* (AI Tech & Kukoon), and the *facilities*—avoiding heavy reliance on individual faculty profiles. 

## 2. Target Audience & Brand Positioning
The funnel is built for our primary persona: **Mrs. Priya Sharma (38, Upper-Middle Class, dual-income)**. She seeks academic rigor balanced with strong values and global readiness, avoiding both "exam factories" and overly "play-focused" schools.

### Core Brand Philosophy to Highlight
*   **The "Head, Hand, and Heart" Approach:** Emphasizing holistic development, critical thinking, and moral character.
*   **Competitive Edge:** Blending traditional values with future-ready tech (AI) that no other local school offers.

### Split Funnel Targeting
*   **Target A: Junior Wing (Kukoon The Preschool, PG to 1st)**
    *   *Focus:* Nurturing environment, foundation building, "play more, grow" philosophy.
*   **Target B: Senior Wing (7th to 10th Class)**
    *   *Focus:* Advanced AI classes, academic excellence, "future-ready citizens."

## 3. Core Deliverables (What needs to be built)
1.  **High-Converting Landing Page (Mobile-First):**
    *   **Hero Section:** Strong hook ("Bright Future Public School: Academic Excellence, Rooted in Values.") with split pathing for Junior vs. Senior wings.
    *   **Value Proposition Section:** Highlight the AI curriculum (Senior) and Kukoon activities (Junior) as institutional systems that guarantee results regardless of individual teaching staff.
    *   **Lead Magnet Integration:** Offer a free download of the *"Reclaim Focus: Smart Device Rules for Academic Growth"* guide in exchange for their name, phone, and child's age. (This leverages the school's digital wellness thought leadership).
2.  **Call-to-Action (CTA) Mechanisms:**
    *   **Primary CTA 1:** Direct "Click-to-Call" buttons to Admissions: **9251414913** and **951414914**.
    *   **Primary CTA 2:** Social integration to Instagram (`@bfpsjaipur`) and Facebook (`https://www.facebook.com/BFPSjaipur`).
    *   **Lead Capture Form:** A clean, minimal form for inquiries and lead magnet downloads.

## 4. UI/UX & Design System (Strict Adherence Required)
The frontend must strictly follow the "Enlightened Minimalism" trend and the GTS Ai CreatEditDesign v1.1 framework.

*   **Grid & Layout:** Use an 8-point grid system for all margins and padding. Ensure generous negative space to convey prestige and clarity.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary Blue: `#004A8F` (Deep Ocean Blue - use for primary buttons and headers).
    *   Secondary Green: `#2A625A` (Forest Teal Green - use for secondary accents and Junior wing themes).
    *   Accent Gold: `#EBA83A` (Warm Amber Gold - use for highlights, stars, or special CTA text).
    *   Backgrounds: `#F8F8F8` (Off-White Cloud) for main backgrounds to keep it airy.
    *   Text Color: `#333333` (Charcoal Grey) for high readability without the harshness of pure black.
*   **Typography:**
    *   Headings: **Merriweather Bold** (Conveys academic authority and tradition).
    *   Body & Buttons: **Open Sans Regular / Semibold** (Clean, modern, highly legible).

## 5. Technical Specifications
*   **Frontend:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS (configured with the custom color palette and fonts above), Vanilla JS.
*   **Backend/Integration:** Lightweight Node.js/Express server OR a Google Apps Script webhook to push form submissions (Name, Phone, Child's Class, Intent) directly to a Google Sheet.
*   **Tracking:** Include placeholders for Facebook/Instagram Meta Pixel and Google Analytics tags.

## 6. Success Metrics
*   Increase in overall student enrollment (Target: +200 students).
*   Conversion rate of landing page visitors to Lead Form submissions (downloading the Screen Time guide).
*   Volume of direct admission calls generated.
